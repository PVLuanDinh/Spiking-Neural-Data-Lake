## Owner directions and interpretation

The owner wants a brain built from a structural snapshot to continue learning through synaptic plasticity during sleep. The owner then clarified that sleep should be longer, human-sleep-inspired and capable of rewiring eligible circuits throughout the learned brain to improve capability. The snapshot fixes the starting reference, not all later weights or topology. "Entire brain" means older learned circuits, newer growth and eligible projections may participate; it is not a requirement to alter every connection. The owner also asked how individual cortical columns and neurons should be designed to solve tasks. A column-sized growth batch is a computational unit, not one neuron or a literal biological cortical column, and its problem lineage is not permanent task ownership.

## Sleep: inspiration, implementation and falsification

Human sleep repeatedly alternates NREM and REM, with deep NREM generally earlier and REM periods longer later. Those stages inspire hypotheses, not a claim of biological equivalence or a fixed software eight-hour timer. Human intervention and animal studies support coordinated replay, selective strengthening/weakening, experience-linked spine formation/elimination and selective forgetting. They do not establish that every synapse changes nightly or that more sleep computation always improves a task. Sources: [NHLBI sleep stages](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep/stages-of-sleep), [human coordinated sleep events and memory intervention](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-023-01324-5), [sleep-related spine formation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4447313/), [REM-related spine refinement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5535798/), [selective synaptic scaling](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28154076/), [REM-active cells and forgetting](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7378274/).

One existing scheduler runs extended, repeatable, resumable sleep bouts:

1. **NREM-inspired consolidation:** replay typed eligible lake episodes through the actual learner, interleaving recent and older experience. Eligible synapses may change. Selective homeostatic normalization is a separate ablation arm, never an assumed benefit.
2. **REM-inspired integration:** combine or vary already known experience for generalization. Mark internal variations as synthetic; they never become observed facts, rewards or labels. Propose targeted formation, removal or retention of connections through the existing growth, anatomy and validation admission rules.
3. **Assessment and save:** choose candidates on development validation, enforce prior/rare-task retention, and journal accepted weights, topology and continuation state in the one lake. A fresh process must reproduce the saved capability and resume the interrupted stage.

Sleep has no new teaching input, motor side effects or advancement of wake chronology during the experiment; queue incoming external events for wake. It can sharpen and recombine prior experience, not discover absent external facts. Record participating populations and connections to substantiate brain-wide eligibility, rather than counting cycles as coverage. Protected scaffolds, hardware safety and anatomical admission rules remain binding.

Use measured work budgets for wall time, compute, replayed events, synaptic updates, structural candidates, coverage and backlog. Compare short, medium and extended budgets under matched compute; compare staged and flat replay. Pause at the registered budget, sustained validation plateau, interference limit or wake request, preserving stage, cursor, RNG, local learning traces, pending candidates and pressure baseline. Duration alone is never success.

Required matched arms from the same presleep state: no replay, uniform replay and staged replay at equal compute; plasticity ON/OFF with topology held fixed; structural plasticity ON/OFF with matched replay and compute; stage order intact/shuffled if claiming an order benefit; short and extended budgets. Judge new-task generalization, old/rare-task retention, stability, actual resource cost and fresh-process restore with learning OFF during evaluation. Split source sessions before indexing or tuning, use paired seeds and a separate untouched final test. Leakage, extra-update-only gains, old-skill regression and non-restorable gains are RED. The no-replay and valid-restart controls must run through the actual consumer, not a mocked counter.

Plan placement: P4d states the owner directive; R2/T103 adds stage/budget and interruption-resume state to its existing typed replay contract; R3/T104 distinguishes synaptic from structural gains and compares stage/budget controls; G6/T114 expands structural eligibility beyond the newly solved problem's units while preserving joint-mask validation, untouched tests and provenance; G-R6 gains direct capability, interference, fabrication and restore reds.

## Growth unit: reusable collaborating microcircuits

The owner evidence proposes a column-sized growth batch of roughly 8–10 excitatory neurons plus shared inhibition, with size as an experimental arm. Primate task studies find distributed, context-dependent mixed representations, a reason to test reusable collaborating batches rather than presume one batch per task: [Mante et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature12742), [Rigotti et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature12160). This is a design hypothesis, not proof that SNDL's layout will win.

The current `src/snn_data_lake/cortex/cortical_microcircuit.py` is a Potjans–Diesmann-like dynamics reference of roughly 620 cells at its scaled default. It has a scalar thalamic rate port, no task training, and fresh simulation state on each call; spontaneous layer statistics are not cognitive competence ([original model](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23203991/)). The existing `src/snn_data_lake/growth/neuron_zoo.py` has LIF/ALIF and a reduced dendritic candidate, but its dendritic adapter supplies zero apical current. A separate causal context port and a context-disconnected control are required before claiming contextual dendritic computation. Dendritic evidence makes the arm plausible, not mandatory for every cell: [human dendritic recordings](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31896716/), [segregated-dendrite learning model](https://elifesciences.org/articles/22901).

Minimal candidate: persistent patterned timestamped input spikes; LIF and ALIF as matched cell arms; sparse recurrent excitatory/inhibitory wiring with measured silence/saturation/stability; separate feedforward and context/feedback channels; synaptic eligibility traces plus an explicit delayed teaching/modulatory signal; stable neuron identities, position, layer, sign and lineage; spikes or declared state summaries through the G3 feed projection; the G5 return projection tested separately. Adaptive inhibition is a challenger against fixed inhibition ([Vogels et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22075724/)). E-prop motivates a possible recurrent local-credit rule, with trace definitions matching chosen cell dynamics; it is neither a settled cortical mechanism nor an automatic solution to every task ([Bellec et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-17236-y)).

Port contract: input carries source IDs, timestamps, simulation units, encoder version and episode boundaries, preserving patterns; context is distinct and cannot leak the target/future into inference; teaching declares source, timing, target population and validity mask, with inference still running when learning is OFF; outputs enter the reservoir by declared feed synapses; a direct-read arm measures reservoir value; return feedback reaches named participating brain neurons; state declares durable or reconstructible weights, topology, adaptation, traces, delay queues, RNG, lineage and learning cursor.

Deciding tests: G2's delayed-cue/state and nonlinear-capacity cases compare incumbent, equal-size presized nonlinear features, single-neuron growth, column-batch growth and random attachment under matched final size and search cost. A matching presized arm defeats a growth-specific claim. Ablate carried state, ALIF, context port, adaptive inhibition and plasticity against simpler matched arms. Learn A and B, then a composition task; compare shared collaborating batches with task-exclusive batches and neuter cross-batch projections. Compare reservoir feed with direct readout and norm-matched random feed; neuter the return path. Evaluate prospective held-out gain, old/rare-task retention, cost, and fresh-process restore. A firing-pattern match or unit count alone does not pass.

Incremental path: persistent patterned input, trainable shared batch, causal credit assignment, G2/G3 comparisons, reservoir/return integration, then extended sleep refinement. Detailed dendrites and anatomical wiring are challengers whose extra cost must earn a behavioral benefit.

## Fly-connectome examples and claim boundary

[Janelia Male CNS](https://www.janelia.org/project-team/flyem/male-cns-connectome) supplies an anatomical reconstruction. [Lulzx/fly-brain](https://github.com/Lulzx/fly-brain) separates wiring from strengths and plasticity; its engram demonstration tests recall after teaching, not sleep learning. [DOOM-x-Fly](https://github.com/Aur1ety/DOOM-x-Fly) reports Doom play with a fixed connectome and trained output layer; [DoomFly](https://github.com/shreyash-sharma/doomFly/blob/main/docs/how-it-works.md) likewise trains its readout while the connectome stays frozen. [DOOMFLY's learning lab](https://fly-brain-doom.awormuth.chatgpt.site/learning) permits selected existing synapses to change but reports survival learning unproven. These show that a fixed starting wiring map can take part in new behaviors. They are not evidence that SNDL's own sleep process has learned a skill.

## Literature check of the sleep scope (owner-ordered, 2026-09-29)

The owner asked for a webgrab investigation of "rewire the entire brain" versus "eligible learned circuits". A read-only research agent fetched each source below. For PMC full texts it used webgrab's stealth-browser tier past a "cookies must be enabled" page (no CAPTCHA was solved); abstracts were re-confirmed on PubMed. Sleep-linked change appears wherever it was examined, so eligibility is brain-wide, but it is graded and selective. Size-dependent downscaling (about 18% in mouse M1/S1) spares large synapses; REM prunes new, not established, spines; replayed, tagged or co-active synapses are spared or strengthened; the sign differs by region. Neither every-synapse rewiring nor learned-circuits-only scope fits. Longer sleep buys complete NREM-to-REM cycles and several replay waves, not unbounded gain: half a night matched a full night on two tasks, and no fetched study tests sleep far beyond one night. So end a bout on a yield or homeostatic criterion and repeat bouts. P4d's operational reading follows this (every plastic circuit eligible, change selective by strength, activity and tags). Open: the structural data come from young mice and few areas; downscaling and sleep-dependent potentiation are unreconciled; fly evidence is markers and circuit assays, not wiring. Sources: [de Vivo 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28154076/), [Li 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28092659/), [Yang 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24904169/), [Tononi & Cirelli 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24411729/), [Klinzing 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31451802/), [Havekes 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27549340/), [Durkin & Aton 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26285006/), [Stickgold 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10771409/), [Mednick 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12819785/), [Bushey 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21700878/), [Donlea 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21700877/), [Dag 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30801246/).
