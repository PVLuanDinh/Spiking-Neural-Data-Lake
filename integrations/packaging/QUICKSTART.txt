SPIKING NEURAL DATA LAKE - QUICKSTART
=====================================

WHAT IS THIS?
A neuromorphic brain that runs continuously, plus a spiking data lakehouse.
Desktop app for Windows 64-bit.  No Python install required.

HOW TO RUN
1. Unzip this archive to a folder.
2. Double-click snn-ui.exe.

   A console window opens and the brain starts ticking. Once it is up, your
   default browser opens the COCKPIT at http://127.0.0.1:8137/ -- if it does
   not, open that address yourself. The console keeps printing the boot
   banner, the cockpit URL, and a per-cycle learning line; closing it stops
   the brain.

THE COCKPIT - ONE PAGE, ONE BRAIN
  The whole frontend is a single web page served by the running brain. There
  are no tabs. It shows:

  Anatomy view    The brain in 3D: one dot per module, coloured and sized by
                  its region's live spike rate. The lines between regions are
                  the REAL typed connectome, not decoration. Drag to steer the
                  camera; double-click to stop it. The rotation is a camera
                  and nothing else moves without data.

  Arm + workspace The simulated 2-link arm's pose and the workspace cells it
                  has reached. No hardware is connected or driven.

  Hardware target A 6-DOF torque budget from the build notes: required vs
                  rated kg-cm per joint, and the 12V supply headroom. This is
                  a PLAN, not telemetry -- the page labels it NOT BUILT, and
                  nothing there is measured.

  Live telemetry  Motor mix, behaviour and emotion, neuromodulators, world
                  model, and the bottom strip: coverage, reach, stability,
                  affect, global access, life (nights slept), lake census.

  Chat            Type to the brain. Your turn is queued and answered by the
                  thread that owns the tick loop, so the reply takes as long
                  as the brain takes.

  Anything the brain cannot supply renders as an em-dash, never as a zero
  bar. Anything it can supply but that does not mean what a live bar implies
  (frozen at construction, sleep-only, a literal) is drawn with a provenance
  tag beside it.

WHAT IT REMEMBERS
  Learning accumulates in a durable home at %USERPROFILE%\.snn_data_lake.
  Close the app and reopen it and the brain resumes -- it does not start over.
  Only one brain may run at a time; a second launch detects the lock, exits,
  and its browser tab shows the cockpit of the one already running.

SMOKE TEST (No Display)
  Verify the app without a browser or display:
    snn-ui.exe --smoke
  Serves the cockpit page, checks it against the brain's telemetry contract,
  round-trips one chat turn, and exits 0. Writes nothing durable.

SAFETY NOTES
  The arm is SIMULATED. This app connects to no hardware and drives none.
  Motor commands pass through collision detection and EMERGENCY_STOP.
  The cockpit binds 127.0.0.1 only -- it is a window onto a local process,
  never reachable from another machine.
