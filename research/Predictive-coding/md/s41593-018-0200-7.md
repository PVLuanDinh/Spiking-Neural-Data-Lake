‘strange inversion’, in which the brain became an organ of inference, actively constructing explanations for what’s
going on ‘out there’, beyond its sensory epithelia. One paper played a key role in this paradigm shift.

kinase C (PKC) activity, Colgan et al. identify PKCα  as critical for integrating NMDA receptor and neurotrophin
signaling to control dendritic spine structural plasticity, synaptic potentiation, and learning and memory.
